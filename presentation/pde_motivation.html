Why not an ODE?
An ODE tracks one variable at a time — a point evolving. Temperature at a single location. A PDE tracks the whole field simultaneously.
The surface u(x, t)
Every point on this surface is a measurement: temperature at position x, at time t. The PDE is the law that shapes this surface.
α — diffusivity
Controls how fast heat spreads through space. A single parameter, yet it sculpts the entire space-time landscape. This is what we want to infer.
