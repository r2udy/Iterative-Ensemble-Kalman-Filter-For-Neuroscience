What is experimental data? Physics
Real measurements are never clean. A thermocouple reading at position xᵢ gives:
ũ(xᵢ, t) = u(xᵢ, t) + εᵢ
where εᵢ ~ 𝒩(0, σ²) captures:
  • Sensor calibration error
  • Electrical interference
  • Contact thermal resistance
  • Finite sensor response time
Random residuals → model is correct.
Systematic residuals → model assumption is wrong.
